# Credits

## Pixel art: Pop Shop Packs

All cat and bird sprites in this package are by **Pop Shop Packs**
([itch.io/pop-shop-packs](https://pop-shop-packs.itch.io/)) and are included with the
artist's permission.

| Sprites | Pack | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 50 cat colour variants | [Cats &#124;&#124; Pixel Asset Pack](https://pop-shop-packs.itch.io/cats-pixel-asset-pack) | `sprites/cats/*.png` |
| 12 garden bird species | [Garden Birds &#124;&#124; Pixel Asset Pack](https://pop-shop-packs.itch.io/garden-birds-pixel-character-asset-pack) | `sprites/birds/*.png` |

The sprites are **not** covered by this package's MIT licence. They remain under the
artist's own terms (see the pack pages). In short:

- use in your projects, personal or commercial, and modify them
- do not resell or redistribute them as stand-alone assets
- do not turn them into NFTs or feed them into generative AI tools
- credit is appreciated: link to <https://pop-shop-packs.itch.io/>

Changes made for this package: files renamed (e.g. `black_0.png` → `black-0.png`,
`spritesheet_blue jay.png` → `blue-jay.png`) and losslessly re-compressed. Pixels are unchanged.

If you like the art, please support the artist: the packs are pay-what-you-want.

All meows and bird calls are synthesised in code (`src/audio/`); no recorded audio is shipped.
