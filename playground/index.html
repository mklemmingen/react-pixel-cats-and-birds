react-pixel-cats-and-birds playground

Visual checks for the sprites, the behaviour and the synthesised sounds. Run npm test for the automated suite.

1. Cat animations, all 8 directions

Each idle pose plays in, freezes, then plays out (the label shows the phase). Walk and run loop. Rows are S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE.

2. Bird animations

Every species: flying, pecking, resting, hit. Click a bird to hear its call.

3. Live scene

Cats and birds stacked behind content. Click a cat (meow, it gets up and runs) or a bird (chirp, it bolts). Clicks that miss the birds reach the cats underneath.

Content sits on top

4. Sound board

Every meow preset and every bird call, with a live spectrogram (0-10 kHz).

Sprites: Cats and Garden Birds pixel asset packs by Pop Shop Packs, used with permission.
